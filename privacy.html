Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Transparency and data protection are fundamental to our client partnerships.

1. Information We Collect

BrightMark collects information that you voluntarily provide to us when submitting inquiries through our contact forms, subscribing to our email newsletters, or requesting proposal audits. This may include your full name, work email address, phone number, company name, and project scope details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information solely for legitimate business purposes:

  • Responding directly to your inquiries and preparing tailored marketing proposals.
  • Delivering requested marketing insights, newsletters, and agency updates.
  • Improving website performance, navigation ease, and user experience.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations and industry standards.

3. Cookies & Analytical Technologies

Our website may utilize standard first-party and third-party cookies (such as Google Analytics) to analyze aggregate visitor traffic patterns, bounce rates, and device resolutions. These technologies do not extract personal identification without your knowledge. You can disable cookies at any time via your browser settings.

4. Information Sharing & Third Parties

BrightMark does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We share information only with trusted cloud infrastructure providers necessary to host our website and dispatch requested email communications.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/SSL) and administrative access safeguards to ensure that submissions remain protected against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal contact records. You may unsubscribe from any email communications at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link or contacting us directly at privacy@brightmarkagency.com.

7. Contact Inquiries

For questions regarding this policy or our data practices, please write to us at privacy@brightmarkagency.com.
