Paid Acquisition

Google Ads & PPC Management

Capture customers at the exact moment they are looking for your products with hyper-targeted paid search and display funnels.

Profitable Customer Acquisition, Engineered for ROI

Pay-Per-Click advertising provides immediate exposure to in-market buyers, but without disciplined keyword filtering and bid architecture, ad spend can easily be wasted.

BrightMark creates rigorous multi-tiered PPC architectures. We combine negative keyword scrubbing, Performance Max asset groups, responsive search ads, and tailored landing pages to systematically reduce your customer acquisition cost (CAC).

What We Deliver

Search Advertising

High-intent Google Search campaigns, exact and phrase match grouping, negative keyword exclusions, and responsive search ad testing.

Display Advertising

High-impact Google Display Network banners, dynamic retargeting, YouTube pre-roll video ads, and contextual placements.

Campaign Optimization

Weekly manual bid adjustments, smart bidding strategy tuning (tCPA, tROAS), ad copy variant tests, and dayparting schedule optimization.

Conversion Tracking

Server-side Google Tag Manager setup, enhanced conversions in GA4, CRM pipeline attribution, and real-time ROAS dashboarding.
