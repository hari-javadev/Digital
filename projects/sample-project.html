NovaPay Fintech Redesign

Transforming a complex digital payments interface into a sleek, responsive customer acquisition engine with streamlined registration flows.

Project Overview

NovaPay provides cross-border B2B digital payment gateways for mid-market merchants. While their backend transaction engine was robust, their client-facing marketing portal suffered from legacy code bloat, ambiguous value propositions, and a multi-step signup questionnaire that suffered an 82% abandonment rate.

Client Goals

  • Redesign the complete web portal to establish market trust and institutional credibility.
  • Shorten onboarding friction and reduce lead drop-off on mobile devices.
  • Improve organic search visibility for high-intent B2B merchant queries.
  • Achieve sub-second page load times on standard 4G connections.

The Challenge

NovaPay required extensive regulatory disclosure documentation and security certifications on the website without overwhelming prospective business users. The design needed to balance high regulatory compliance with effortless modern consumer-grade UX.

The Solution

BrightMark engineered a component-based frontend architecture leveraging modern Bootstrap 5 and vanilla JavaScript. We restructured the information architecture into three distinct merchant tiers, built an interactive pricing calculator, and reduced onboarding to a progressive 2-step verification model.
